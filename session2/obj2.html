Session 2 - Objectives

By the end of this session, students will be able to analyze multi-modal transport trade-offs, master technical cause-and-effect structures, execute timed TOEIC strategies, audit real-world mobility corridors, and prepare for the Formative Evaluation.

1

Analyze Multi-Modal Transport (DDRS)

Evaluate infrastructure trade-offs along a 600 km corridor using life-cycle emissions, land take, and territorial equity.

2

Express Cause, Effect & Proportionality

Accurately apply formal prepositions (owing to, as a consequence of) and double comparatives (The + comparative..., the + comparative...) in B2/C1 technical reports.

3

Master TOEIC Workplace Strategies

Identify high-frequency transport collocations and correlative inversions (No sooner... than) under timed reading conditions.

4

Resolve Real-World Mobility Dilemmas

Audit corporate travel routes and formulate actionable modal-shift recommendations.

5

Track Formative Evaluation (FE) Readiness

Demonstrate fluency through targeted circumlocution games and log self-study targets for the upcoming FE assessment.

6

Integrate DD&RS Principles into Professional Travel

Connect global travel scenarios to concepts of sustainable mobility, cultural respect, and responsible tourism.

7

Prepare for the Class 5 Formative Evaluation (FE)

Understand the exact assessment criteria—vocabulary, fluency, grammar, and TOEIC-readiness—for the upcoming 30-minute graded speaking activity that determines their "Go / No-Go" status.
