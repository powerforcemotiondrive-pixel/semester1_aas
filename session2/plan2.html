GSEA Semester 1 Lesson Outline

Session 2 Lesson Plan (2 Hours)

Step-by-step 2-hour structural breakdown covering historical transit evolution, multi-modal corridor debates, technical cause-and-effect grammar, TOEIC inversion drills, corporate transit audits, and Formative Evaluation (FE) tracking.

0-15
1.

Warm-Up & Rapid Retrieval (15 mins)

"Timeline Clash" — Historical shifts in mobility and their socio-environmental costs.

15-40
2.

Media & Critical Analysis (25 mins)

"The Multi-Modal Pitch" — 3 vs. 3 debate evaluating infrastructure trade-offs along a 600 km regional transit corridor.

40-60
3.

Grammar in Context (20 mins)

Expressing cause, consequence (owing to, resulted in), and complex comparison (The more... the...) in technical report writing.

60-80
4.

TOEIC Workplace Application (20 mins)

Strategy, high-frequency transport collocations, and correlative inversion structures (No sooner... than) in Parts 5 & 7.

80-95
5.

DDRS Case Discussion (15 mins)

"The Transit Audit" — Resolving corporate travel dilemmas (short-haul flight vs. TGV) and formulating modal-shift actions.

95-110
6.

Consolidating Game (15 mins)

"The Taboo Modal Shift" — Rapid circumlocution drill and active retrieval of session transport vocabulary.

110-120
7.

Debrief & Tracking (10 mins)

Self-study logging, target setting, and verification of Formative Evaluation (FE) speaking assessment readiness.
