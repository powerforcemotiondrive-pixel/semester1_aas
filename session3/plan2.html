GSEA Semester 1 Lesson Outline

Session 3 Lesson Plan (2 Hours)

Step-by-step 2-hour structural breakdown mastering Toulmin argumentation structures, persuasive rhetoric, advanced hedging discourse markers, and high-stakes environmental policy debates.

0-15
1.

Warm-Up & Logic Diagnostics (15 mins)

"Premise to Conclusion" — Detecting logical fallacies, emotional appeals, and unsupported claims in climate editorials.

15-40
2.

Toulmin Argumentation Workshop (25 mins)

Structuring debate points using Claim, Evidence, Warrant, Backing, Counter-claim, and Rebuttal frameworks.

40-60
3.

Grammar in Context (20 mins)

Advanced hedging devices and nuanced concession markers (Notwithstanding, Arguably, While it is acknowledged that...).

60-80
4.

TOEIC Workplace Application (20 mins)

Disagreement and nuance detection in corporate board meetings and sustainability oversight hearings.

80-95
5.

DDRS Oxford-Style Debate (15 mins)

"Should short-haul commercial domestic flights be banned when high-speed rail connections exist under 3 hours?"

95-110
6.

Rebuttal Rapid-Fire (15 mins)

Formulating spontaneous, grammatically controlled counter-arguments within 60-second turn constraints.

110-120
7.

Debrief & Self-Assessment (10 mins)

Peer review of persuasive speaking metrics and feedback on rhetorical clarity.
