GSEA Semester 1 Lesson Outline

Session 3: Debate and Critical Thinking

Essential professional and technical vocabulary covering operational concepts, analytical frameworks, and formal communication structures.

Single-Pilot Operations (SPO)

01

A proposed commercial aviation operational framework replacing two human pilots with one pilot assisted by certified AI systems.

Autonomous Flight Deck

02

A cockpit architecture where flight control, navigation, and systems management are managed primarily by autonomous software.

Liability Allocation

03

Legal determination of financial and criminal responsibility among operators, airframers, and software developers following a crash.

Machine Ethics

04

The engineering discipline embedding moral decision rules into automated systems facing unavoidable catastrophe scenarios.

Workforce Disruption

05

The displacement of high-skilled labor (pilots, dispatchers) resulting from rapid adoption of automated systems.

Cognitive Complacency

06

A psychological hazard where human pilots become overly trusting of automated systems, dulling manual flying reflexes.

Situational Awareness

07

A pilot's real-time perception and understanding of aircraft status, flight envelope, and atmospheric surroundings.

Fail-Operational Architecture

08

A design standard ensuring a system continues safe execution even following an unexpected component failure.

Trolley Dilemma in Aviation

09

Philosophical thought experiment analyzing algorithmic prioritization between passenger safety and ground population casualties.

Certification Dilemma

10

The regulatory challenge of certifying non-deterministic machine learning models under traditional aerospace safety protocols.

Automation Bias

11

The tendency for humans to favor suggestions generated by automated systems over their own contradictory sensory evidence.

Discourse Concession

12

Acknowledging the validity of an opponent's critique before presenting a superior counter-rebuttal in technical debate.

Social Equity in Automation

13

Ensuring that efficiency gains from automation are shared equitably rather than concentrating profits while eliminating jobs.

Human Factors Engineering

14

Designing technology interfaces tailored to human psychological capabilities and sensory limitations.

Air Traffic Control Autonomy

15

Transitioning tactical separation assurance from human air traffic controllers to centralized automated algorithms.

Redundant Flight Architecture

16

Multiple independent software and hardware systems working in parallel to vote on control inputs during flight.

Regulatory Oversight

17

Governmental safety supervision verifying that automated innovations meet rigorous legal and moral benchmarks.

Pilot Union Advocacy

18

Collective bargaining and public safety advocacy by flight crew organizations defending two-pilot safety margins.
