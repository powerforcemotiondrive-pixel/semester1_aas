Session 3 - Overtourism Objectives

By the end of this session, students will be able to demonstrate measurable academic and operational proficiency across technical vocabulary, grammatical accuracy, structured argumentation, and Formative Evaluation benchmarks.

1

Assess Tourism Carrying Capacity (DDRS)

Evaluate the physical, ecological, and psychological limits of tourist destinations and identify indicators of destination saturation.

2

Formulate Policy Solutions for Crowd Management

Analyze regulatory mechanisms including dynamic visitor taxation, advance reservation systems, and dispersed transit routing.

3

Produce Hypothetical & Inverted Conditionals

Construct advanced inverted conditional structures (Were the council to limit access..., Had mitigation strategies been implemented...) in policy debates.

4

Execute Timed TOEIC Reading Strategies

Accurately parse complex municipal transit regulations, tourism board annual reports, and economic impact data under exam conditions.

5

Synthesize Sustainable Tourism Trade-Offs

Balance the economic vitality of hospitality-dependent local economies against heritage degradation and resident housing pressures.
