Session 1: Extreme Weather

Essential professional and technical vocabulary covering operational concepts, analytical frameworks, and formal communication structures.

Microburst

01

An intense localized downdraft of air that spreads radially upon ground impact, creating hazardous low-altitude wind shear.

Clear-Air Turbulence (CAT)

02

Severe air turbulence occurring in cloudless skies near jet streams, undetectable by conventional radar.

Runway Visual Range (RVR)

03

The horizontal distance down a runway that a pilot can see centerline markings and approach lights.

De-Icing Fluid

04

A glycol-based chemical solution sprayed onto aircraft surfaces to melt ice and prevent aerodynamic degradation.

Ground Stop

05

An air traffic control directive pausing departures destined for an airport experiencing hazardous weather conditions.

Convective Storm Cell

06

A localized thermal updraft producing cumulonimbus clouds, severe precipitation, lightning, and hail.

Jet Stream Oscillation

07

Shifts in high-altitude ribbon winds that alter flight durations, fuel consumption, and transatlantic route corridors.

Runway Excursion

08

An incident where an aircraft inadvertently departs the runway surface during takeoff or landing maneuvers.

Hydroplaning

09

Loss of tire traction on standing runway water, causing the aircraft to skid uncontrollably upon touchdown.

Diversion Airport

10

An alternative airport designated to receive flights unable to land at their scheduled destination due to weather.

METAR Report

11

A standardized aviation routine weather report issued hourly, detailing wind velocity, visibility, and cloud cover.

Holdover Time (HOT)

12

The estimated duration anti-icing fluid will prevent frost or snow from accumulating on treated aerodynamic surfaces.

Microclimate

13

Localized climatic conditions differing significantly from surrounding geographical areas, common near airport valleys.

Inversion Layer

14

An atmospheric condition where temperature increases with altitude, trapping fog and pollutants over runway areas.

Wind Shear Alert

15

An automated cockpit or tower warning detecting sudden changes in wind velocity and vector along the flight path.

Precipitation Attenuation

16

The reduction of radar signal strength caused by heavy rainfall, concealing severe storm cells behind initial precipitation.

Thermal Runway Softening

17

The deformation of asphalt paving caused by extreme surface temperatures during unprecedented summer heatwaves.

Resilience Hardening

18

Engineering upgrades fortifying airport drainage, sea walls, and terminal structures against extreme climate events.
