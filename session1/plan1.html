GSEA Semester 1 Lesson Outline

Session 1 Lesson Plan (2 Hours)

Step-by-step structural breakdown incorporating professional icebreakers, TOEIC diagnostics, vocabulary building, structured discussions, and evaluation criteria.

0-15
1.

Introductions & Icebreaker (15 mins)

Students complete "The Professional Two Truths & One Goal" activity in pairs to set learning objectives and build rapport.

15-45
2.

TOEIC Strategy & Diagnostics (30 mins)

Overview of test structure, scoring, Part 5/6/7 examples, and pacing tips to transition from general English to exam technique.

45-65
3.

Vocabulary Builder (20 mins)

Introduction to essential professional travel collocations, airport procedures, and DD&RS vocabulary (carbon footprint, eco-certification).

65-110
4.

Structured Discussion & Grammar (45 mins)

Students share travel anecdotes integrating DD&RS themes, followed by a guided review of narrative tenses and inversion structures (Hardly had I...).

110-120
5.

Debrief & Wrap-up (10 mins)

Class recap, explanation of the Final Evaluation (FE) criteria for Class 5, and completion of personal takeaways.
