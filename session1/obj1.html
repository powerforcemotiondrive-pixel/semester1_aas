Session 1 - Objectives

By the end of this session, students will be able to master the TOEIC exam structure, professional collocations, advanced grammar, sustainable travel principles, and formative evaluation criteria.

1

Understand the TOEIC Exam

Understand the structure, scoring system, and strategic pacing of the TOEIC Listening & Reading test, shifting from general language study to targeted exam technique.

2

Master High-Frequency Travel & Professional Collocations

Identify and correctly utilize essential B2/C1-level travel collocations and idioms in professional and conversational contexts.

3

Produce Advanced Narrative Structures

Review and accurately apply narrative tenses (Past Simple vs. Past Continuous) and inversion structures to enhance complexity in speaking and writing.

4

Integrate DD&RS Principles into Professional Travel

Connect global travel scenarios to concepts of sustainable mobility, cultural respect, and responsible tourism.

5

Prepare for the Class 5 Formative Evaluation (FE)

Understand the exact assessment criteria—vocabulary, fluency, grammar, and TOEIC-readiness—for the upcoming 30-minute graded speaking activity.
